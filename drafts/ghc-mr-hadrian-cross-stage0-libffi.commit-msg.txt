hadrian: no target libffi for cross stage0 ghci

Since b2721819f3, hadrian passes configure's libffi dirs, which are the
target's, to ghci at every stage. Stage0 of a cross build is built for
the build platform, so linking the stage1 compiler can pick the
target's libffi. Master avoids this since 23c9b6c392. Fixes #NNNNN.
